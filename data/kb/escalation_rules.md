# Escalation Rules

## Escalate to a human immediately when
- The customer reports a full outage, data loss, or a security or privacy incident.
- The ticket mentions legal action, a regulator, a chargeback, or press.
- An enterprise-tier customer reports anything that blocks their production use.
- The requested refund or credit exceeds $500, or an enterprise contract term is in question.
- The customer states they intend to cancel or has asked to be escalated already.
- Two previous replies have failed to resolve the same issue.

## Severity and first-response targets
| Severity | Definition | Enterprise | Pro | Free |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Production down, no workaround | 30 minutes | 4 hours | best effort |
| S2 | Major feature broken, workaround exists | 2 hours | 8 hours | best effort |
| S3 | Minor issue or question | 1 business day | 2 business days | best effort |
| S4 | Feature request or feedback | 3 business days | 5 business days | best effort |

## Do not escalate when
The answer exists in the knowledge base and the customer has not previously been given it, the
issue is a known limitation with a documented workaround, or the request is a routine feature
suggestion from a free or pro account.

## Tone on escalation
Acknowledge the impact explicitly, state what happens next and who owns it, and give a concrete
time for the next update rather than a promise to resolve.

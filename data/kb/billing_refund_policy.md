# Billing & Refund Policy

## Billing cycles
Subscriptions renew automatically on the anniversary of the signup date. Monthly plans
are charged on the same calendar day each month; annual plans are charged once per year
and include a 15% discount versus monthly pricing.

## Refund eligibility
- **Duplicate charges** are always refunded in full, automatically, once verified. No approval needed.
- **Unused time after cancellation** is refunded pro rata for annual plans only. Monthly plans
  run to the end of the paid period and are not refunded.
- **Refund window**: refund requests must be raised within 60 days of the charge date.
- **Enterprise contracts** are governed by the signed order form; contact the account manager
  before promising a refund.

## Failed payments
A failed payment triggers three retries over ten days. The workspace becomes read-only after
the third failure and is fully restored the moment a valid payment method succeeds. Data is
never deleted for non-payment inside 90 days.

## Invoices and VAT
Invoices are available under Settings → Billing → Invoices. VAT is applied based on the billing
country recorded on the account; adding a valid VAT number removes VAT for eligible EU customers
and reissues the invoice within one business day.

## Escalation
Any disputed amount above $500, or any chargeback notice, must be escalated to the billing
operations team rather than resolved by an agent.
